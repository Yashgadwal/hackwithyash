How to Secure Your Teen's Instagram & Social Accounts

July 7, 2026 • By TrackMaster Safety Editorial

Instagram is one of the most popular social media platforms among teenagers. While it allows creative self-expression and connection with friends, it can also expose young users to account compromise, fake impersonators, and cyber harassment if accounts are not properly secured.

As a parent or guardian, setting up robust account privacy and security configurations helps prevent unauthorized logins and protects your teen's personal privacy.

5 Essential Security Measures for Teen Accounts

  1. Activate Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Require a verification code via an authenticator app (like Google Authenticator) on every new login attempt.
  2. Switch to Private Account Mode: Ensure only approved followers can see photos, reels, and story updates.
  3. Turn Off Activity Status: Disable the "Show Activity Status" toggle so unfamiliar accounts cannot observe when your teenager is active online.
  4. Filter Inappropriate Direct Messages: Enable Instagram's "Hidden Words" feature to automatically filter offensive comments and message requests.
  5. Supervise Connected Apps: Regularly remove permissions granted to third-party follower trackers or quiz apps that collect login credentials.

Parental Supervision Tip

Remind your teenager that social media accounts should never be used to share home addresses, school locations, or travel itineraries in real-time. Use TrackMaster to keep track of digital safety alerts and ensure a safe online journey.
