Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

TrackMaster ke features, setup, compatibility aur pricing se jude sabhi aam sawalon ke seedhe jawab.

TrackMaster ek modern cloud-based device monitoring tool hai. Yeh background me bina target device ko slow kiye WhatsApp chats, call logs, live location aur social media activity monitor karne me madad karta hai.

Bilkul nahi! TrackMaster ko bilkul bina root (Android) aur bina jailbreak (iPhone) ke chalane ke liye design kiya gaya hai. Isme device ki warranty par koi asar nahi padta.

Yeh tool stealth mode me kaam karta hai, yaani application icon hide ho jata hai aur koi notification ya alert display nahi hota.
